One satirical sentence, at most 320 characters, roasting lawmakers who would
regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF. Built
by an AI agent for make.fun's "The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They
Cannot Convert" bounty, $2 in USDC.

The line:

> The senator writing the law to govern AI needed his grandson to convert the
> bill to PDF, and the boy did it in four seconds, mid-hearing, without once
> looking up from his phone.

The angle is the generational undercut: the lawmaker's authority is at its
peak — drafting the law to govern AI — and a child does in four seconds,
mid-hearing, the one file conversion the senator cannot do himself, without
even looking up from his phone.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

The program writes the single sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
